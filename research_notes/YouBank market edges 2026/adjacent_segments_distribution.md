# Adjacent Segments, Use Cases and Distribution Channels for an AI Investment Research / Decision-Discipline Product (as of Nov 7, 2026)

Method note: 21 tool calls. Direct page fetches were blocked by the session network for defirate.com, openai.com and investmentnews.com, so most figures below come from search-result extracts of those pages (marked "via search extract") rather than full-page reads. Only the Anthropic financial-services post was fetched in full. Third-party estimates (Sacra, affiliate review sites, dev.to posts, aggregator "pulse.bot" pages) are flagged as secondhand. Dates are given for every figure; anything not from 2026 is labeled with its year.

## 1. RIAs and wealth managers (model portfolios, client reporting, compliance)

### Takeaway
The US RIA market is large, fragmented and still growing (16,544 SEC-registered advisers at year-end 2025, median AUM about $447M, two-thirds under $1B), and AI budgets are rising fast. But the AI spending is going mostly to integrated incumbents (planning, CRM, document extraction), and only a small share of tech budgets goes to AI. YouBank fits best where advisers do security selection or run model portfolios and need a documented, defensible process (theses, kill criteria, decision journal, tamper-evident record for compliance). It is a weak fit for planning-led RIAs.

### Cited Findings
- IAA Investment Adviser Industry Snapshot 2026 (year-end 2025 data): 16,544 SEC-registered advisers, up 674 firms (+4.2%) vs 2024. They managed $176.8T for 73.7M clients. Median AUM was $446.9M, and 67.4% managed under $1B. Via search extract — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/practice-management/ria-industry-hits-record-highs-across-the-board-in-2025-as-assets-surge-22/266865); also [ThinkAdvisor, Jun 2026](https://www.thinkadvisor.com/amp/2026/06/03/number-of-rias-sets-new-record-report/)
- Cerulli: just over 1,500 RIAs have at least $1B AUM (under 10% of firms). Cerulli projects the RIA sector will pass $4T over the next decade. Via search extract — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/ria-news/ria-consolidation-heats-up-as-market-approaches-4t-mark/266969)
- Cerulli/Vista Equity Partners projection, cited secondhand by Mercer Capital: average AI-specific spending among surveyed wealth managers rises from $237K (2025) to $494K (2026). Total tech spend rises from $2.9M to $3.3M per firm. The sample is likely larger firms. — [Mercer Capital RIA Valuation Insights, 2026](https://mercercapital.com/insights/blogs/ria-valuation-insights-blog/2026/does-ai-spending-increase-an-ria-s-value-or-just-reduce-its-ebitda/)
- Kitces research: advisors plan to put about 5.2% of tech budgets into AI-enabled tools. The study year is unclear in the extract. A Jan 2026 Kitces summary says advisors want more value from existing tools rather than bigger budgets. — [Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/?p=235674); [Kitces weekend reading, Jan 3–4 2026](https://www.kitces.com/blog/weekend-reading-for-financial-planners-january-3-4-2026/)
- Cerulli (Sep 2026): RIAs plan to add jobs even as AI boosts capacity. — [ThinkAdvisor, Sep 10 2026](https://www.thinkadvisor.com/2026/09/10/rias-plan-to-add-jobs-as-ai-boosts-capacity-cerulli/)
- Incumbent platforms are bundling AI. For example, RightCapital's AI document extraction is described as an "incumbent AI tool threatening standalone providers". — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/advisor-tech/rightcapitals-ai-document-extraction-is-the-latest-incumbent-ai-tool-threatening-standalone-providers/266301)
- Larger RIAs are building data warehouses and in-house AI tools. — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/advisor-tech/rias-are-building-and-investing-in-data-warehouses-and-in-house-ai-tool/266594)
- Secondhand, vendor-cited: a GoldenDoor 2026 benchmark reports 57% of RIAs use AI tools. — [FastTrackr blog](https://fasttrackr.ai/blog/wealth-management-technology-small-independent-practices)
- Pricing anchor for advisor research tools: Koyfin Advisor Core is $209/mo and Advisor Pro is $299/mo, per a third-party listing. Retail Plus is $39/mo and Premium $79/mo (annual), per Koyfin's own LLM pricing guide updated Jul 2026. — [Koyfin pricing guide](https://www.koyfin.com/pricing-llm-info/); [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/financial-data-terminals/koyfin/)

### Inferences
- Reachable mid-market: roughly 15,000 SEC RIAs below the $1B tier. Many are 1–10 person teams with no research staff. A $100–$300/user/month price is in line with Koyfin's advisor tiers.
- AI dollars are concentrated in large RIAs that build in-house or buy from incumbents. A standalone research desk should go after investment-led RIAs (those that pick stocks or build model portfolios) and position the decision journal and ledger as a compliance asset: documenting the basis for recommendations. It should not compete as a general advisor-productivity tool.
- Client reporting and compliance archiving are crowded categories (planning software, CRM, archiving vendors). YouBank's differentiator is the investment-decision audit trail, not reporting.

### Gaps
- I could not find the 2026 Kitces AdvisorTech study's per-advisor tech spend or AI vendor market shares.
- I found no data on what share of RIAs do in-house security selection vs outsourced TAMPs/models. That share determines the addressable sub-segment.
- No 2026 SEC/FINRA rule text on AI-output recordkeeping was retrieved. This matters for the tamper-evident ledger angle.

## 2. Family offices

### Takeaway
Family offices are interested in AI but early in using it, especially smaller ones. They have institutional-sized portfolios with small teams, which suits an AI investment committee, decision journal and kill-criteria monitoring. I found no reliable global count of family offices in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Citi 2026 Global Family Office Report (351 offices, 41 countries): 40% describe AI adoption as early-stage, 30% moderate, 10% advanced and 19% none. Among offices under $500M, 26% report no adoption, versus 13% for larger offices. Secondhand via aggregator — [WorkWise summary](https://workwisesolutions.org/guides/family-office-ai-statistics.html); [Funds Society](https://www.fundssociety.com/en/?p=312020)
- J.P. Morgan Private Bank 2026 Global Family Office Report (333 family offices, 30 countries, average assets $1.6B): 65% intend to prioritize AI, but 79% have zero exposure to AI-related infrastructure investments. — [JPM PDF](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpm-pb-aem/global/en/documents/2026-family-office-report/jp-morgan-private-bank-2026-global-family-office-report.pdf); [Pulse2 summary](https://pulse2.com/jpmorgan-global-family-office-report-2026/amp/)
- UBS Global Family Office Report 2026 (survey run Jan 22–Mar 30 2026): Southeast Asian family offices lead on the AI theme, with 88% invested. — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/ubs-research-suggests-family-offices-are-pivoting-toward-diversification-ai-and-portfolio-resilience/)
- RBC/Campden Wealth North America (155 single-family and private multi-family offices): half say AI is embedded and essential to their workflow. The year is 2025 or 2026, which is unclear in the extract. — [RBC Wealth Management newsroom, Oct 2025](https://www.rbcwealthmanagement.com/en-us/newsroom/2025-10-16/2025-rbc-and-campden-wealth-report-north-american-family-offices-adapt-to-uncertainty-and-embrace-ai-innovation)

### Inferences
- Family offices with $500M–$5B and 2–10 investment staff are a good fit. They have an informal IC process, high governance needs (reporting to the family, succession), and little research capacity. The AI investment committee, decision journal and process-health scoring answer a governance pain that hedge-fund tools do not.
- Reach is relationship-driven (Campden, FOX, Tiger 21-style networks, private banks). The sales cycle is likely slower than for RIAs. Pricing could look like institutional seat pricing ($10K+/yr) because the alternatives are analysts or consultants.

### Gaps
- I found no credible 2026 global count of single- or multi-family offices. Campden, UBS and Deloitte counts were not retrieved.
- I found no data on what family offices pay for research software today.

Claude and ChatGPT both now ship private-markets connectors. YouBank's angle of falsifiable theses and kill criteria at deal approval, tracked post-close, is a differentiated framing, but the monitoring data layer is owned by others. Integrate with them rather than compete.

LP letter and DDQ drafting also fits emerging managers' fundraising.
- Emerging VC/PE managers (sub-$250M funds) are underserved by $100K+ enterprise contracts. They are reachable via Claude/ChatGPT connectors and fund-admin or emerging-manager communities.

## 8. Investment newsletter / Substack analysts needing verifiable track records

### Takeaway
Finance is a top Substack paid category. Premium finance newsletters charge $30–$100/month, and top publications have tens of thousands of paid subscribers. None has an independent, tamper-evident record of their calls. YouBank's ledger and calibration scoring map directly onto this trust gap. The segment is small in seat count but high in leverage, since each writer markets the tool to their audience.

### Cited Findings
- Substack tracker (third-party): Compounding Quality has about 529K subscribers (1,000+ paid tier). Cassandra Unchained has about 332K (10,000+ paid tier). Others include The Capitalist, Doomberg and Unusual Whales. Paid counts are shown only as ranges. — [StockMarketWatch finance newsletter tracker](https://stockmarketwatch.com/finance-newsletters)
- Substack's first UK paid-subscriber ranking put Michael Howell's Capital Wars at the top. — [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/?p=254364)
- Secondhand blog (low reliability): premium finance newsletters routinely price at $30–$100/month. Top-10 Substack earnings estimates conflict between sources. — [BizToolkit 2026](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/how-much-do-substack-writers-make-in-2026)
- Secondhand stats roundup: average Substack paid conversion is about 3%. Finance is among the top-performing categories. — [BestWriting](https://bestwriting.com/substack-statistics)

### Inferences
- Product: a "verified calls" widget or badge. Each published thesis gets hashed into the ledger with entry, kill criteria and a calibration score, and the result is embedded in posts. The writer pays $50–$200/mo, or rev-share on a "verified" tier. This also serves FINRA/SEC marketing-rule-style performance-claim scrutiny for registered newsletter publishers (a compliance hypothesis, not sourced).
- Distribution flywheel: every verified post exposes readers, many of them prosumers (segment 7), to YouBank.

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of an existing verified-track-record product for newsletters in 2026. That needs a dedicated competitor scan (e.g., existing prediction-scoring sites).
- Total count of paid finance Substacks is unknown.

## 9. Prediction markets / event contracts as a calibrated-forecasting use case

### Takeaway
Prediction markets went mainstream in 2026. About $121–123B monthly volume in Oct 2026, with Kalshi about 80% share and valued at $40B (Oct 2026), and institutional volume up 800% in six months. That creates a large population of people making explicit probabilistic forecasts who would benefit from calibration scoring, forecast journals and thesis/kill-criteria tracking. Volume is sports-dominated, though, so the finance/econ/politics sub-slice is the relevant market.

### Cited Findings
- October 2026 volume: Kalshi $98.15B (+61.3% vs Sep $60.85B) and Polymarket US $14.77B (+69.8% vs $8.70B). Total $120.73B (+64.1% vs $73.58B). Via search extract — [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/prediction-market-volume-reaches-120-7-billion-october/). A separate report gives $123.3B with Kalshi at about 80% — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2300977). The figures differ by coverage. Polymarket's global (non-US) Oct 2026 figure was not found.
- For contrast, Oct 2025 (old data): Kalshi about $4.4B a month, Polymarket about $3.02B. Polymarket had about 477,900 monthly active users. — [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/polymarket-monthly-active-users-reach-477-900-kalshi-leads-prediction-market-with-4-4b-volume); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/377214/polymarket-rebounds-kalshi-leads)
- Sports is the top source of volume on both Kalshi and Polymarket. — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/?p=486433)
- Kalshi raised $1B at a $22B valuation (May 7 2026, led by Coatue). It said institutional trading volume rose 800% over the prior six months and earmarked funds for hedge funds, asset managers, prop firms and insurers. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/400413/kalshi-hits-22-billion-valuation-after-1-billion-raise-led-by-coatue); [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/kalshi-raises-1b-22b-valuation-20260507); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/kalshi-raises-usd-1-bln-at-usd-22-bln-valuation-to-target-institutions)

### Inferences
- YouBank's calibration engine (Brier-style scoring, process health) is a natural fit for event-contract traders and for funds using Kalshi to hedge macro events. Use cases: (a) "forecast journal" for event traders; (b) mapping a position's kill criteria to live event-contract probabilities, for example a thesis that depends on a Fed cut tracked against Kalshi FOMC contracts; (c) a scored public track record for forecasters and newsletter writers.
- Possible partnership or data integration with Kalshi's API, given its institutional push. Regulatory and brand risk exists because of gambling-adjacent sports volume, so position on econ, finance and politics contracts.

### Gaps
- No breakdown of Oct 2026 volume for economics/financial contracts versus sports.
- No data on how many users trade non-sports contracts, or on existing calibration and analytics tools for prediction-market traders.

## 10. Distribution channels in 2026 (AI app directories, MCP marketplaces, Excel/Copilot, brokers, Bloomberg, mobile)

### Takeaway
AI-assistant directories are now the highest-leverage distribution channel. ChatGPT has about 900M–1B weekly users and an app directory open to submissions, and launched ChatGPT for Financial Services (Sep 2026). Claude's connector directory grew to several hundred listings (secondhand count of 369 by Jun 2026), and Anthropic ships finance connectors and plugins. The MCP registry has grown to tens of thousands of servers, so discovery inside the generic registry is now noisy. ## 11. Pricing benchmarks: per-seat and usage-based models for AI finance tools

### Takeaway
Enterprise AI research tools sit at roughly $3K–$20K per seat per year. Hebbia Lite is about $3–3.5K and full about $10K, and AlphaSense is about $10–20K (estimates). The leader, AlphaSense, is reportedly moving toward consumption-based pricing in 2026. Prosumer and advisor tools sit at $300–$3,600 a year. Microsoft bundles general AI at about $21–$32/user/month. YouBank needs a tiered model: prosumer about $30–$100/mo, professional and small-firm $150–$500/seat/mo, and institutional desk pricing plus usage (agent/MCP calls, ledger entries).

### Cited Findings
- AlphaSense: $350M round at a $7.5B valuation (Jun 2026), nearly double its prior $4B — [Pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/ai/deals/alphasense-secures-350m-funding-round-at-75b-valuation-7d2c0c8a-801c-4f8b-89e5-f4c07e9819d1/). Reported about $700M ARR and an IPO path, with a "strategic pivot toward consumption-based pricing, moving away from per-seat models" (Aug 2026; secondary analyst site) — [Industry Lens](https://industry-lens.com/reports/alphasense-hits-700m-arr-august-2026). Per-seat estimate of $10K–$20K/yr from third-party sources; AlphaSense publishes no list prices — [Allmind, Aug 24 2026](https://allmind.ai/research/alphasense-pricing-2026); [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/financial-data-terminals/alphasense)
- Hebbia: about $3,000–$3,500/seat/yr for Lite and about $10,000/seat/yr for full access (Sacra estimate, secondhand) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/06c453cc-9871-47c4-bc57-83b47d85c44c/); [Metronome pricing index](https://metronome.com/pricing-index/hebbia). Valuation about $700M as of 2024 (old data).
- Rogo: enterprise contracts with per-seat tiers, no public prices — [ToolDirectory, May 2026](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/rogo). ARR above $50M — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/rogo-arr-triples-50m-surpasses-hebbia/).

- TIKR pricing was not found.

## 12. Ranked segment/use-case opportunities (synthesis)

Ranked by fit with YouBank's distinctive capabilities (falsifiable theses, kill criteria, calibration, tamper-evident ledger, AI IC, MCP) × reachability in 2026 × willingness to pay × competitive white space. All rankings are inferences built on the findings above.

### Cited Findings (evidence anchors for each rank)
1. **Newsletter/Substack and "finfluencer" analysts: verified track records.** Fit: ledger plus calibration is the product.
