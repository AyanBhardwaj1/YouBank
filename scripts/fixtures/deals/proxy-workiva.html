SUMMARY

This summary highlights selected information from this proxy statement and may not contain all of the information that is important to you.

The Merger Consideration (page 60)

If the merger is completed, each share of Company common stock will be converted into the right to receive $42.50 in cash, without interest, which represents a premium of approximately 31.4% to the closing price of the Company common stock on March 1, 2024, the last trading day before the public announcement of the merger. The total equity value of the transaction is approximately $2.1 billion and the transaction implies an enterprise value of approximately $3.4 billion, including the assumption of net debt.

Opinion of Northgate Partners LLC (page 41)

Northgate Partners LLC rendered its oral opinion, subsequently confirmed in writing, that the merger consideration was fair, from a financial point of view, to the holders of shares.

Financing of the Merger (page 58)

Parent has obtained committed debt financing from Bank of Atlas, N.A. and Meridian Capital Markets LLC. The obligations of Parent are not subject to a financing condition.

Termination Fees (page 88)

The Company will be required to pay Parent a termination fee of $68 million under specified circumstances, and Parent will be required to pay the Company a reverse termination fee of $136 million under specified circumstances.

The Parties to the Merger

Harbor Ridge Midstream, Inc. Harbor Ridge is a natural gas gathering and processing company with operations in the Delaware Basin of West Texas and southeastern New Mexico. Harbor Ridge owns approximately 1,900 miles of gathering pipelines and four cryogenic processing plants with aggregate capacity of 860 million cubic feet per day. The Company's principal executive offices are located in Houston, Texas.

Lantern Infrastructure Partners Fund IV, L.P. Parent is a Delaware limited liability company formed by investment funds managed by Lantern Infrastructure Partners LLC solely for the purpose of entering into the merger agreement. Lantern is a private equity firm focused on energy infrastructure.

Background of the Merger

The board of directors of the Company regularly reviews the Company's strategic alternatives. On June 12, 2023, a representative of Lantern Infrastructure Partners contacted Mr. Doyle, the Company's chief executive officer, to discuss a potential acquisition of the Company.

At the direction of the board, Northgate contacted 25 potential counterparties, consisting of 14 strategic parties and 11 financial sponsors, regarding their interest in a potential transaction with the Company.

Over the following weeks, nine parties entered into confidentiality agreements with the Company, including Lantern.

On October 3, 2023, the Company received non-binding indications of interest from five parties, with values ranging from $36.00 to $41.00 per share.

On January 18, 2024, the Company received final bids from two parties: Lantern, at $42.50 per share in cash, and Party B, at $40.75 per share in cash.

On March 3, 2024, the Company, Parent and Merger Sub executed the merger agreement, and the Company issued a press release announcing the transaction before the opening of trading on March 4, 2024.

Reasons for the Merger; Recommendation of Our Board of Directors

In reaching its determination, the board considered that the merger consideration of $42.50 per share represents a premium of approximately 31.4% to the closing price on March 1, 2024, and the certainty of value of an all-cash transaction.

The board also considered the Company's prospects as a standalone company in the Delaware Basin, including commodity price exposure and the capital required for a fifth processing plant.

Opinion of Northgate Partners LLC

The Company retained Northgate Partners LLC to act as its financial advisor. Northgate will receive a fee of approximately $31 million, of which $3 million became payable upon delivery of its opinion and the remainder is contingent upon the consummation of the merger.

Termination Fees

The Company has agreed to pay Parent a termination fee of $68 million if the merger agreement is terminated because the board changes its recommendation or the Company accepts a superior proposal.

Parent has agreed to pay the Company a reverse termination fee of $136 million if the merger agreement is terminated because Parent fails to consummate the closing when required.

Regulatory Approvals Required for the Merger

The merger is subject to the expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976. Harbor Ridge and Parent filed their HSR notifications on March 22, 2024, and the waiting period expired on April 22, 2024.
