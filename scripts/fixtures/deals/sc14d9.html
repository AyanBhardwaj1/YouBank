Item 1. Subject Company Information.

The name of the subject company is Quillstone Therapeutics, Inc., a Delaware corporation.

Background of the Offer and the Merger

On April 4, 2025, representatives of Ardent Biopharma contacted Quillstone to express interest in a strategic transaction.

Quillstone, with the assistance of Fenwick Row & Co., contacted 18 potential acquirers. Six parties entered into confidentiality agreements with Quillstone.

On June 30, 2025, Quillstone received final proposals from two parties.

On July 14, 2025, Quillstone, Ardent and Purchaser entered into the merger agreement, under which Purchaser commenced a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares for $27.00 per share in cash plus one non-transferable contingent value right per share, which represents the right to receive $4.00 in cash upon the first regulatory approval of QS-301 in the United States.

Reasons for the Recommendation

The offer price of $27.00 per share represents a premium of approximately 64% to the closing price of the shares on July 11, 2025.

Opinion of Fenwick Row & Co.

Fenwick Row performed a sum-of-the-parts discounted cash flow analysis, resulting in an implied per share equity value range of $22.60 to $31.40.

Termination Fee

Quillstone will be required to pay Ardent a termination fee of $52 million in specified circumstances, which represents approximately 3.5% of the equity value of the transaction.
