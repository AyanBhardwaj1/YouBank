DEFINITIVE PROXY STATEMENT

QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS ABOUT THE SPECIAL MEETING

Q: What will I receive in the merger? A: You will receive $18.00 in cash and 0.4125 shares of Corvane common stock for each share of Bellweather common stock.

INFORMATION ABOUT THE COMPANIES

Bellweather Software Corporation provides cloud-based scheduling and workforce management software to hospitals and clinics. Bellweather is headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina.

Corvane Health Systems, Inc. is a publicly traded provider of healthcare information technology listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market.

MERGER CONSIDERATION

At the effective time, each share of Bellweather common stock will be converted into the right to receive $18.00 in cash, without interest, and 0.4125 shares of Corvane common stock. Based on the closing price of Corvane common stock on September 6, 2024, the merger consideration represented approximately $36.60 per share and a premium of approximately 27% to the closing price of Bellweather common stock on that date.

BACKGROUND OF THE MERGER

On February 2, 2024, the chief executive officer of Corvane called the chief executive officer of Bellweather to propose a combination of the two companies.

During the process, representatives of Hollis & Grant contacted twelve (12) parties, of which eight (8) parties executed confidentiality agreements.

Three parties submitted written indications of interest by May 15, 2024.

On September 8, 2024, Bellweather and Corvane executed the merger agreement.

OPINION OF THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL ADVISOR

Bellweather engaged Hollis & Grant LLC as its financial advisor in connection with the merger.

Hollis & Grant's discounted cash flow analysis indicated an implied per share equity value range of $31.20 to $40.90.

EXPENSES AND TERMINATION FEES

The merger agreement provides for a "go-shop" period: until 11:59 p.m. on the date that is forty-five (45) days after the date of the merger agreement, Bellweather may solicit alternative proposals.

Bellweather must pay Corvane a termination fee of $41.5 million, or $20.7 million if the termination relates to a superior proposal received during the go-shop period.

REGULATORY APPROVALS REQUIRED FOR THE MERGER

Completion of the merger requires the expiration of the waiting period under the HSR Act. On November 12, 2024, Bellweather and Corvane each received a request for additional information and documentary material (a "second request") from the Federal Trade Commission.
